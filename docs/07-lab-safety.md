# 7. Laboratory safety

## Mandatory controls

- Use only an isolated, current-limited low-voltage DC bench supply.
- Do not power this educational prototype directly from mains electricity.
- Set the current limit before energising the transmitter.
- Keep metal tools, jewellery, cards and unintended electronic devices away from
  the active field.
- Do not touch coils, MOSFETs, capacitors or rectifiers immediately after testing;
  they may be hot.
- Use capacitors with suitable AC ripple-current and voltage ratings.
- Discharge capacitors before changing connections.
- Do not connect a phone during tuning or fault testing.
- Use a resistive or electronic dummy load first.
- Stop immediately if current rises unexpectedly, waveforms become unstable,
  components heat rapidly, or insulation smells or discolours.

## Resonant overvoltage

The supply may be only 12 V, but voltage across an individual coil or resonant
capacitor can be much higher. Probe ratings, grounding and measurement topology
must be checked before connecting an oscilloscope.

## Oscilloscope grounding

Ordinary bench-oscilloscope ground clips may be connected to protective earth.
Attaching them to a switching node can cause a short circuit. Use an appropriate
differential probe or a safely isolated measurement method approved by the
laboratory supervisor.

## Medical and interference caution

Do not operate the prototype near implanted medical devices or safety-critical
electronics. The project is not certified for electromagnetic compatibility,
consumer charging or unattended use.
